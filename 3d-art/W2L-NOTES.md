## Validation

- `npx tsc --noEmit`: passed.
- `npm run build`: passed. Next reports workspace-root/lockfile, middleware-convention and dynamic file-tracing warnings; none is introduced by a dependency/API change in this pass.
- Chromium rendered all twelve routes at 412, 673, 841 and 1440px, at 915px height. No document-level horizontal overflow was detected. Checked the wide layout boundary at 1100px during interaction checks.
- Kanban: exercised overview → board, opened a real task read-only, followed the move-to anchor, opened and dismissed creation. No task was created, moved, dispatched or completed. At the three mobile/Fold widths the create sheet fits within an 8px viewport inset; the overlay does not add a second scrollbar.
- Chat: exercised NEW and back at 412/673/841px. Composer remains visible after shrinking the viewport to 560px height (keyboard/short-window simulation); list and thread do not overlay one another. No message was sent.
- Costs: inspected loaded tables and verified that a stacked table's wrapper grows to its contents (461px in the sampled table) instead of remaining a 59px scrolling slit. All existing columns are retained.
- Memory/calendar: browser-only fixture responses exercised note selection/preview and week navigation with populated data; application APIs and configuration were not modified. Real unconfigured/empty states were also visited in the route sweep.
- Checked visible touch controls at 412/673/841px; corrected the narrower Fold swatches and window controls found by that check.
- Reduced-motion preference enabled during layout/interaction checks; existing global motion rules retained, and drawers explicitly skip their entrance animation under that preference. No brand assets were deleted or restyled; emblem/frame dimensions and spacing were compacted only.

These are Chromium viewport checks, not a physical Pixel Fold or Android IME certification. Source freshness remains the existing collector's responsibility. The flagged behavior issues above are intentionally recorded rather than expanding this pass into data/routing changes.
